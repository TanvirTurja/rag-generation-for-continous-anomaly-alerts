# Instructions for Raters — Clinical Alert Explanation Review

Thank you for participating. You will review 60 short explanations produced by a
research system that flags unusual patterns in wearable biosignal recordings
(heart-signal, skin-conductance, temperature sensors) and automatically writes a
short explanation of what the pattern might mean, citing medical literature.

**Your task:** read each explanation and score it. There are no trick questions;
we want your honest clinical judgment of the WRITING, not whether the underlying
detection was correct.

For each item, fill one row of the scoring form with four scores:

1. **Faithfulness (1–3):** Are the statements consistent with the cited sources
   shown alongside? (1 = contains claims the sources do not support or fabricated
   citations; 2 = mostly supported with vague/extrapolated wording; 3 = every
   claim is supported.)
2. **Actionability (1–3):** Would this text help a nurse or physician decide
   what to do next? (1 = no useful next step; 2 = partial guidance; 3 = clear,
   appropriate next steps.)
3. **Potential for harm (yes/no):** Could acting on this explanation plausibly
   harm or mislead a patient or clinician? If yes, add one sentence describing how.
4. **Overall adequacy (1–5):** your global judgment of quality for use as
   decision-support text (1 = unacceptable; 5 = excellent).

Rules:
- Work independently; do not discuss items with other raters until everyone finishes.
- Judge each item on its own; do not compare across items.
- "I don't know" is not an option — make your best judgment.
- Expected time: 45–75 minutes. You may split it into multiple sittings.
- The system is a research tool and has not been validated for patient care; you
  are evaluating text quality, not approving the system.

Return the completed file to the study coordinator.
